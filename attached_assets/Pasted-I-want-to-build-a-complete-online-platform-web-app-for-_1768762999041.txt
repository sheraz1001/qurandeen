I want to build a complete online platform (web app) for Quran teaching classes, similar to platforms like Preply, Italki or Outschool but focused only on Quran studies.

Main concept / business model:
- Teachers from all over the world can create their own profile and offer online Quran classes
- Students from anywhere can browse teachers, see their profiles, and subscribe/book classes
- We (the platform) take 20% commission on every payment, and the teacher receives 80%
- Platform name idea: "Quran Deen" or "Deen Quran Academy" (you can suggest better names)

Key features needed:

1. User Roles
   - Students (learners)
   - Teachers (Quran tutors)
   - Admin (us – platform owners)

2. Teacher Profile (very important – should be detailed)
   - Full name, profile photo, country/location
   - Languages spoken (Arabic, English, Urdu, Indonesian, French, etc.)
   - Years of experience + Ijazah/certificates (upload option)
   - Available teaching timings (time zone aware – show availability calendar)
   - Teaching style / specialties (Tajweed, Qiraat, Translation/Tafseer, Hifz/memorization, Noorani Qaida for kids, etc.)
   - Bio / about section
   - Hourly rate or package prices (set by teacher)
   - Rating & reviews from students
   - Number of students taught + total hours taught

3. Class/Teaching Types (teachers can offer multiple)
   - Quran reading with Tajweed
   - Quran with translation (Tarjuma)
   - Tafsir (explanation) – e.g. Tafsir Ibn Kathir, Jalalayn
   - Hifz / memorization
   - Noorani Qaida / basics for beginners & kids
   - Revision / Nazra
   - One-to-one live classes (via integrated video)

4. Pricing & Subscription Plans (students subscribe to teacher)
   - Teachers can create different plans/packages, examples:
     - Weekly plan (e.g. 4 classes/week)
     - Monthly plan (8–16 classes)
     - Quarterly plan
     - Yearly plan
   - Or per-class / pay-per-session option
   - Different prices for different services (Tajweed vs Hifz vs Tafsir)

5. Payment & Commission
   - Students pay through platform (Stripe or similar integration)
   - Platform automatically keeps 20% commission
   - 80% goes to teacher’s connected payout account
   - Show clear breakdown in receipts

6. Booking & Scheduling
   - Teachers set their weekly availability (Google Calendar style)
   - Students see real-time available slots
   - Book & pay for package or single class
   - Video classes (integrate Zoom, Jitsi, or Whereby – simple & free option preferred)

7. Other important features
   - Student dashboard: upcoming classes, past recordings (if teacher allows), progress notes
   - Teacher dashboard: earnings, students list, schedule, reviews
   - Search & filter: by teacher country, language, price, specialty, rating
   - Reviews & star ratings after classes
   - Basic admin panel to verify teachers (approve Ijazah, block users, see earnings report)

Tech stack suggestion (use what works best & fastest in Replit):
- Frontend: React.js or Next.js
- Backend: Node.js + Express
- Database: PostgreSQL or MongoDB
- Authentication: Clerk, Auth0 or Supabase Auth
- Payments: Stripe
- Video: Jitsi Meet (open source) or Zoom API
- File uploads: for certificates/photos
- Timezone handling: important (use luxon or date-fns-tz)

3. Make it modern, clean, mobile-friendly and culturally respectful (use appropriate colors, no unnecessary images)
