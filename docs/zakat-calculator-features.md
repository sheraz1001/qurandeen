### ⚖️ Accurate Nisab Calculation

The calculator uses authentic Nisab thresholds based on Islamic jurisprudence:

- **Gold Nisab:** 87.48 grams (approximately 7.5 tola or 3 ounces)
- **Silver Nisab:** 612.36 grams (approximately 52.5 tola or 21 ounces)
- **Dynamic Calculation:** Uses the lower of gold or silver Nisab for threshold determination
- **Customizable Prices:** Enter current gold/silver prices for real-time accuracy

## How to Use

### Step 1: Set Currency & Prices
- Select your preferred currency
- Enter current gold price per gram
- Enter current silver price per gram

### Step 2: Confirm Hawl
- Check the box if your wealth has been held for one lunar year

### Step 3: Add Your Assets
- Click on each category to expand
- Click "Add Item" to enter your assets
- Provide a description and value for each item

### Step 4: Add Any Debts
- Add debts you owe in the "Debts You Owe" category
- These will be automatically deducted

### Step 5: View Results
- See your total assets, deductions, and net zakatable wealth
- View Nisab status (met or not met)
- Get your exact Zakat amount if obligatory
