What is Hadith?

Hadith refers to the recorded sayings, actions, and approvals of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him). These collections serve as the second most important source of Islamic law and guidance after the Holy Quran. The hadith literature provides practical examples of how to implement Quranic teachings in daily life.
